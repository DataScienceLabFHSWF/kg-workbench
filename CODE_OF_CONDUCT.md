# Code of conduct

We welcome participation regardless of experience, identity, background, or
disability. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on
ideas and code. Assume good intent while taking the impact of your words seriously.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual advances, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information without consent
are unacceptable. These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests,
discussions, and spaces where someone represents the project.

To report a concern, contact a repository maintainer through a private contact
method listed on their GitHub profile. If none is available, request a private
contact channel without posting personal details or the incident publicly.

Maintainers will assess reports, respect the privacy of those involved, and take
action appropriate to the situation. This may include removing content, issuing
a warning, or temporarily or permanently restricting participation. Maintainers
are subject to the same expectations.
